About Aerotropolis Mohali

Looking for an Aeretropolis LOI for sale? Aerotropolis is GMADA's premier aviation township on the Airport Road. We connect buyers and sellers trading Letters of Intent (LOIs) for residential (100–500 Sq Yd) and commercial properties in Blocks A–J.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an Aerotropolis LOI?

A Letter of Intent (LOI) is an official document issued by GMADA, granting the holder rights to a residential or commercial plot within the Aerotropolis township before physical possession is transferred.

How does this marketplace work?

Select your intent (Buy or Sell), choose your block allocation (A-D or E-J), and pick your property size. The tool instantly connects you with verified market participants to execute a transparent transaction.
