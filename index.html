Invest in GMADA Aerotropolis: Mohali's Aviation Township

Looking for an Aeretropolis LOI for sale? Aerotropolis Mohali is GMADA's 7th independent visionary township, spanning over 5,500 acres near the Shaheed Bhagat Singh International Airport. Our dedicated marketplace connects buyers and sellers trading Letters of Intent (LOIs) issued under the land pooling policy for villages like Bakarpur.

Available Block Allocations & Plot Sizes

  • Blocks A to D: A rapidly developing zone with comprehensive infrastructure. Block A features residential plots up to 2,000 sq yards, while Blocks B through D cap residential inventory at 500 sq yards. Commercial opportunities include 25 sq yard booths and up to 200 sq yard showrooms.
  • Blocks E to J: Premium expansion zones sharing dimensions with Eco City 3. Designed for substantial builds, offering 200, 300, and 500 Gaj residential options alongside large-format commercial sites.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a GMADA Aerotropolis LOI?

A Letter of Intent (LOI) is an official instrument issued by the Greater Mohali Area Development Authority. It grants the holder the right to a residential or commercial plot within the Aerotropolis township before physical possession is transferred.

How does Aeretropolis LOI Bazaar work?

Simply select your intent (Buy or Sell), choose your block cluster (A-D or E-J), and pick your property size. The app instantly connects you via WhatsApp to execute a verified, transparent market transaction for your specific Mohali property requirement.
